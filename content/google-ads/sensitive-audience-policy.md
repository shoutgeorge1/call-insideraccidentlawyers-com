# Google Ads — Sensitive Audience Policy (Slip & Fall Funnel)

**Audience:** account managers, marketing leads, anyone setting up
audiences, remarketing, or Customer Match for the new California Slip &
Fall educational funnel.

**Bottom line.** Personal-injury and injury-related content may fall
under Google's restrictions on **personalized advertising for sensitive
categories**, including health, personal-hardship, and similar
situations. Do **not** build personalized ad audiences from a user's
slip-and-fall inquiry. Measurement and offline conversion attribution
remain permitted uses.

---

## Do **NOT** create or recommend

- Google Ads website-visitor **remarketing lists** for these pages.
- **RLSA** (remarketing lists for search ads) audiences based on
  visitors to these injury pages.
- **Customer Match** audiences built from the submitted injury leads
  (email or phone).
- **Custom segments** built from users' injury details.
- **Audience names** containing medical conditions, injury types, or
  legal-case details (e.g. "fractured hip leads", "ER fall visitors").
- **Personalized Display, YouTube, Demand Gen, Gmail, or PMax** audiences
  built from this assessment data.

This is true even if the platform technically allows the upload, because
Google's policy may restrict serving personalized ads to users
identified by these signals.

---

## Allowed strategy

- **Search campaigns** based on the user's active search.
- **Contextual** YouTube placements.
- **Contextual** video keywords.
- **Contextual** topics where permitted.
- **Broad or predefined Google audiences** where allowed by Google
  policy.
- **First-party email communication** the user explicitly requested
  (see `/content/emails/slip-fall/README.md`).
- **Conversion measurement.**
- **Enhanced Conversions for Leads** (see `offline-conversions.md`).
- **Offline import** of qualified leads, signed cases, and similar
  events.
- **CRM lead scoring** used internally, **not** for prohibited audience
  targeting.

---

## Why the distinction matters

- **Measurement** answers "did this ad result in a real lead and
  ultimately a real case?" That is allowed and is the foundation of any
  serious Google Ads optimization strategy for this practice area.
- **Audience targeting** answers "show this ad to people we already know
  inquired about an injury." That is exactly the use case Google's
  sensitive-category policy is concerned about.

The funnel is therefore explicitly designed to **maximize measurement
quality** (lead → qualified → signed via offline import) and to
**minimize reliance on audience-list strategies** for this practice
area.
