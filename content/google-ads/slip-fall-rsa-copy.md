## Negative keyword themes

- DIY / "without a lawyer", "no lawyer", "settle without attorney"
- Workers' comp specifics — `osha`, `workers comp claim`, `cal/osha`, `c-9 form`, `workers comp doctor` (workplace is shown as informational only, but `workers comp lawyer` shoppers should go to a workers' comp campaign instead)
- Criminal / unrelated — `arrest`, `assault charge`, `criminal record`
- Recipes / ingredients — `recipe`, `cooking`
- Image / video search noise — `meme`, `funny`, `tiktok`, `instagram`
- Brand confusion / competitors at the litigator's discretion
- Free legal aid / pro bono — keep on a separate routing strategy

## Validation summary

- All RSA headlines confirmed ≤ 30 characters.
- All RSA descriptions confirmed ≤ 90 characters.
- No superlatives ("best", "top", "expert", "#1") that the firm has not
  separately substantiated.
- No guarantees ("we will win", "we guarantee").
- No fabricated case-value language ("worth millions").
- No medical or "maximum compensation" claims.
- Strong message-match: each RSA points to a page whose H1 reflects the
  ad theme.

## Experiment plan (recommended)

1. Phase 1 (2 weeks): Variant A and Variant B each in their **own**
   research/question ad group. Direct-response ad groups remain on the
   existing English/Spanish lawyer landing pages.
2. Phase 2: once enough lead volume exists, set Google Ads to **optimize
   for qualified leads** via offline conversion import (see
   `offline-conversions.md`).
3. Phase 3: review by location / device. Do not personalize audiences
   from this funnel — see `sensitive-audience-policy.md`.
