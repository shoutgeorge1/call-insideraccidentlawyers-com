Hi {{ first_name }},

This is one of the most common reasons people tell themselves a fall
case is not worth looking into:

> "The danger was right there in front of me. They told me there's no
> case because it was 'open and obvious.'"

That is not quite the full picture. The careful version:

- A clearly visible condition **may** reduce or eliminate a property
  owner's duty to warn.
- It does **not** automatically mean there can never be responsibility.
- Other questions can include whether the owner should have **repaired**
  or **protected against** the condition.
- It can matter whether a person had a **practical reason or necessity**
  to encounter the condition — for example, the only path to the
  bathroom, the cashier, your car, or your apartment.
- Comparative fault and the full circumstances may also matter.
- Only an attorney reviewing the facts can determine how the issue
  applies.

If you were told your case has no chance because the danger was
visible — that is exactly the kind of situation that often deserves a
closer look.
