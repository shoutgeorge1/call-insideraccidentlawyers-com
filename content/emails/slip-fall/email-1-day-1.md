Hi {{ first_name }},

Some of the most important evidence in a California fall case can
disappear before anyone realizes it. A short list, in rough order of how
quickly it can be lost:

**1. Surveillance footage.**
Many stores, hotels, and apartment buildings overwrite their security
video on a short loop — sometimes within a few days. Asking in writing
that the footage be preserved can matter.

**2. The scene itself.**
Spills get cleaned. Cones get moved. Broken stairs get fixed. Photos and
short videos of the area, lighting, entrances, exits, and any warnings
actually in place can be hard to recreate later.

**3. Incident reports.**
If a report was made, request a copy. If it was verbal, write down the
employees and managers you spoke with and what was said.

**4. Witnesses.**
Strangers move on. A first name, a phone number, and a one-sentence note
about what they saw can make all the difference later.
