Hi {{ first_name }},

A short, careful note on something people often ask:

> "How serious does my injury have to be?"

Cases handled on contingency are usually pursued when there is a
**meaningful, documented injury**. That is not the same as saying every
small case is hopeless — but a few hours of soreness with no treatment
is generally harder to pursue than:

- Ambulance or emergency-room care
- Hospitalization
- Surgery
- A fracture or broken hip
- A head injury (including concussion symptoms)
- A back or neck injury
- Continuing limitations or lost work

**Two important things this email is NOT.**

1. It is **not** medical advice. If you are having urgent symptoms,
   please contact an appropriate medical provider or 911.
2. It is **not** a suggestion that you should run out and get extra
   treatment to support a claim. That is not how this works.

What does help:

- Save all medical records you already have (ER, urgent care,
  imaging, specialist visits, physical therapy).
- Keep notes on continuing pain, missed work, and things you can no
  longer do the way you used to.
