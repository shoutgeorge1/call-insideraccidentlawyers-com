May Be A Fit If

  • A product, device, tool, equipment, appliance, battery, medical device, implant, or consumer item caused serious injury
  • There were burns, hospitalization, surgery, permanent harm, organ damage, or death
  • A defect, warning failure, malfunction, unsafe design, or contamination may be involved
  • You have product info, photos, medical treatment, or records

Not Every Case Qualifies

  • Warranty issue only
  • The product did not cause physical injury
  • Refund or customer service complaint only
  • No product details or injury documentation

What To Expect

  1. We ask what product was involved, where it came from, and what happened.
  2. Photos, packaging, receipts, records, and the product itself can matter.
  3. If there may be a path, we explain next steps and whether referral/co-counsel review is appropriate.
