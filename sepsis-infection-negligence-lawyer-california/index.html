May Be A Fit If

  • There was sepsis, severe infection, wound infection, pressure sore infection, or post-procedure infection
  • There was hospitalization, ICU care, organ damage, amputation, or death
  • A facility, nursing home, procedure provider, wound care failure, unsafe condition, or preventable delay may be involved
  • There is a clear timeline and medical treatment

Not Every Case Qualifies

  • Minor infection
  • No medical treatment
  • General medical complaint only
  • Pure doctor malpractice with no PI/referral value unless review indicates otherwise

What To Expect

  1. We ask what infection was diagnosed, where treatment happened, and what failure may be involved.
  2. Facility records, wound photos, procedure records, hospital records, and timelines can matter.
  3. If the facts may fit, we explain whether a legal or referral review makes sense.
