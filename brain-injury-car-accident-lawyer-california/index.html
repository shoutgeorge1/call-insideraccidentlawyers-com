Why This Case Type Is Different

Brain injuries are not always obvious immediately after a crash. Symptoms can show up as headaches, memory problems, dizziness, vision issues, or changes in behavior. A concussion or TBI after a crash may require a serious legal and medical review. The case may depend on treatment history, crash impact, and how symptoms affect daily life.
