Strong Premises Cases Usually Have

  • A serious injury (broken bone, head injury, ER care)
  • A commercial property owner (store, restaurant, complex)
  • Evidence — incident report, photos, witnesses, or cameras
  • Medical treatment soon after the incident

Cases That Are Harder To Pursue

  • No injury or no medical treatment
  • Injuries that happened on the job (workers' comp territory)
  • Private home with limited facts or no evidence
  • Incidents more than 2 years ago
