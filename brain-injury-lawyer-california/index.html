May Be A Fit If

  • You were diagnosed with concussion, TBI, brain bleed, head trauma, or neurological symptoms
  • An accident, fall, unsafe property, vehicle crash, facility incident, or other negligence was involved

Not Every Case Qualifies

  • Minor bump with no symptoms and no treatment
  • Research-only traffic
  • No clear negligent party
  • No medical documentation or treatment path
