Med Spa Cases We Review

This page is for serious or lasting physical harm, not minor dissatisfaction with appearance.

Burns, scarring, or infection
Vision loss or nerve damage
Hospitalization or permanent injury
Filler, Botox-type injection, or laser treatment
Unauthorized provider or med spa negligence
Photos, records, diagnosis, or treatment trail

May Be A Fit If

  • There were burns, scarring, infection, vision loss, nerve damage, hospitalization, or permanent injury
  • Filler, Botox-type injection, laser, cosmetic treatment, unauthorized provider, or med spa negligence may be involved
  • The treatment caused serious or lasting harm
  • You have photos, medical treatment, diagnosis, or records

Not Every Case Qualifies

  • Bad cosmetic result only with no physical injury
  • Minor dissatisfaction
  • Price or refund complaint only
  • No medical treatment or documentation

What To Expect

  1. We do not provide medical advice or say a treatment is inherently dangerous.
  2. We review the legal facts: provider, timeline, injury, records, and recovery path.
  3. Calling is the fastest way to see if a case review makes sense.
