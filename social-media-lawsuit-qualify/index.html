The Lawsuits Are Real. And They’re Getting Bigger.*

This is no longer just a parenting debate. Juries, state officials, and federal courts are now looking at whether social media platforms knowingly exposed young users to serious harm.

Los Angeles

$6 million verdict. A Los Angeles jury found Meta and YouTube liable for harm to a young user and also found malice and misconduct.

New Mexico

$375 million in civil penalties. State officials pushed claims tied to harm involving young users, while broader cases continue against platforms like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Snapchat.

Federal Cases

National litigation is growing. Lawsuits across the country argue these products were designed to keep kids engaged longer, even as concerns about anxiety, depression, sleep disruption, and compulsive use kept mounting.
