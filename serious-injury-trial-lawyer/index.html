What To Do After a Serious Accident

Protect your health and your claim.

Get medical care

Your health comes first. Document treatment and follow doctor’s orders.

Document injuries and accident details

Keep treatment notes, bills, and any photos from the scene.

Avoid early insurance settlement offers

Do not accept a quick settlement before speaking with an attorney about your full damages.

Speak with an attorney

Get a case evaluation before signing anything or settling with insurance.
